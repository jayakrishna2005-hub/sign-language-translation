---

### 🤟 How to Use the Translator:
1. Click **`▶ Start Translation`** (choose Browser WebRTC mode).
2. Position your hand inside the **centered blue scan box**.
3. **Spell Words with Letters**:
   - Sign letters (`A`–`Z`) to form words in the *Word Forming* box.
   - Lower/rest your hand for ~0.8s (or press **Spacebar**) to complete the word.
4. **Translate**:
   - Select your target Indian language (Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, etc.).
   - Click **`🔊 Listen`** to hear audio speech playback.
