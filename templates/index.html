Process

How It Works

Four simple steps from gesture to translated speech — all happening in real-time in your browser.

01
Position Your Hand
Hold your hand inside the centered scan region. The guide box shows exactly where AI is looking.
02
AI Detects Gesture
MediaPipe tracks 21 hand landmarks. The LSTM model analyzes gesture patterns across 30 frames.
03
Gesture is Translated
The recognized label is converted to English text, then optionally translated to your chosen Indian language.
04
Read or Listen
The translated sentence builds up live. Hit Listen to hear it spoken aloud in your selected language.
